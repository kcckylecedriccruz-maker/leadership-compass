## Step 1 – Problem statement
Leadership 360 reviews in small and mid-sized organisations are run with spreadsheets, Google Forms and email. Setting up
who rates whom, chasing raters, keeping feedback anonymous and turning comments into development plans is slow and
error-prone, and raters often don't understand the questions.

## Step 2 – Target user
HR / L&D administrators running leadership reviews for 20–300 employees (primary); employees and managers taking part
(secondary). Context: Philippine workplaces where English and Filipino are both used.

## Step 3 – Current alternatives and their gaps
| Alternative | Gap |
| --- | --- |
| Google Forms + Sheets | Manual rater lists, no anonymity rules, no reports or plans |
| Enterprise 360 tools (e.g. large HR suites) | Expensive, heavy to set up, not tailored to local language |
| Consultants running paper or survey-based 360s | Slow, costly per cycle |

## Step 6 – Differentiating angle
- AI that helps raters **understand items and write observable examples** (English and Filipino), not just a chatbot.
- Evaluations generated from the **org structure**, including skip-level, upward, peers, external raters and outside people.
- **Anonymity and privacy by design**: minimum group size, names removed before AI, first-party analytics.
- **Free to run** for small organisations (Gemini free tier, Supabase and Netlify free tiers).
